Microgrid Energy Management using DRL

1. Project Goal

The objective of this project is to optimise energy usage in a microgrid (a home or small community with solar panels and a battery). The goal is effectively to minimise electricity bills and reduce energy wastage by intelligently deciding when to:

  • Store excess solar energy (Charge).
  • Use stored energy to avoid high grid prices (Discharge).
  • Sell energy back to the grid (Export).

2. The AI Model: Deep Q-Network (DQN)

We chose a Deep Q-Network (DQN), a type of Reinforcement Learning (RL) algorithm.

How it works (Simply put)

Instead of just "predicting" the future, the DQN learns to play a game.

The "Game"
Lowering the electric bill
The "Player"
The AI Agent
The "Score"
The Reward (Savings)

3. Why did we use this model?

A. Sequential Decision Making

Energy management is a sequence problem. If you discharge now, you won't have energy later. DQN assumes future consequences, unlike standard regression models.

B. Delayed Rewards

Sometimes the best action pays off later (e.g., charging at 2 PM for a 7 PM peak). DQN uses a "discount factor" to value future rewards.

C. Complex, Non-Linear Patterns

Simple "If/Then" rules fail in complex scenarios. The Neural Network learns subtle strategies that are hard to program manually.

4. System Inputs & Outputs

Inputs (What it sees)

  • LOAD Current power demand
  • SOLAR Current solar generation
  • PRICE Current grid price
  • SOC Battery State of Charge

Outputs (What it does)

CHARGE (Store Energy)
DISCHARGE (Use/Sell Energy)
IDLE (Do Nothing)

5. Technology Stack & System Architecture

The Microgrid OS is built upon a modern, highly scalable architecture seamlessly integrating AI and web technologies.

Backend & Simulation Logics

  • Python & Flask: Provides the highly concurrent API endpoints and web server mapping.
  • Pandas & NumPy: Handles robust data processing and forecasting analysis.
  • Custom Simulation Engine: Emulates physical constraints of the load dynamics, battery, and grid.

Artificial Intelligence

  • PyTorch: Implements the underlying Deep Q-Network for sequential decision making.
  • Reward Function: Optimizes long-term financial benefits while preserving battery health boundaries.
  • Fallback Rules: Rule-based edge case handling for critical operating modes (e.g., Island Mode).

Frontend & UI

  • Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS: Highly performant dynamic interfaces without bloating framework dependencies.
  • Chart.js: Powers the beautiful real-time data visualizations in the Energy Flow analytics.
  • Glassmorphism UI: Incorporates neon highlights and modern dashboard aesthetics.
