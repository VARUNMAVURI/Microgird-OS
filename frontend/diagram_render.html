Data Validation (Outputs from the 70% Prototype):

The integrated Deep Reinforcement Learning optimization model successfully processes energy datasets containing solar generation, load demand, battery State of Charge (SOC), and grid parameters, and consistently produces optimized energy dispatch decisions to improve microgrid efficiency.

The Microgrid OS web dashboard correctly visualizes these optimization results, instantly displaying real-time system metrics such as energy demand, solar power generation, battery storage levels, transmission flow, and AI-driven energy analytics, enabling administrators and residents to monitor system performance effectively.
